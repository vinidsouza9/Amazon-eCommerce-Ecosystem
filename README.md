# Amazon E-Commerce: Integrated Causal Loop Diagram & Virtuous Cycles

Amazon's model is built on four strategic choices. It opened its marketplace to third-party sellers, created the Prime subscription with bundled content, built its own logistics network with FBA and in-house last-mile delivery, and turned shopper attention into an advertising business. Over time, these choices produced consequences that are slow to build and hard to copy, things like a low-price reputation, lower cost per unit, deep customer loyalty, and an ever-growing base of sellers and buyers. Everything converges on one shared engine, Higher Volume.

## V1 · Marketplace Cycle (the core flywheel)

When Amazon opened its marketplace, sellers came in and brought commission fees and higher revenue with them. That extra revenue lowers cost per unit, which makes low prices possible. Low prices earn Amazon a reputation that pulls in more buyers, and more buyers mean more volume, which attracts even more sellers.

## V2 · Prime Ecosystem Cycle

Prime members get fast free shipping and bundled content, so they are happier and abandon their carts less often. Happy members buy more frequently, and that habit hardens into loyalty, which keeps memberships and order volume growing.

## V3 · Logistics Scale Cycle

Higher volume gives Amazon the capital to build a dense logistics network, bring last-mile delivery in-house, and invest in warehouses and AI. Each package gets cheaper to ship, reliance on third-party carriers fades, and delivery gets faster and more reliable. Customers notice, and volume climbs again.

## V4 · Advertising Cycle

A growing base of sellers and customers creates a bigger ad audience on one side and fiercer seller competition on the other. Click prices rise, advertising revenue and profit follow, and that profit draws in yet more sellers.

## How they interlock

Underneath it all runs the oldest flywheel in the company, more buyers attracting more sellers attracting more buyers. V2 locks in demand, V3 compounds the cost advantage, and V4 converts attention into cash, so a win in any one cycle spins all the others faster through Higher Volume.
